# Google Play listing

**App name** (30 max): Remote for Samsung TV

**Short description** (80 max):
Big, simple remote for Samsung smart TVs. Works over your home Wi-Fi.

**Full description:**

A clear, easy remote for Samsung smart TVs, designed so anyone in the family can use it: big
labelled buttons, no clutter, no ads.

Features
• Finds your TV on the home Wi-Fi automatically, no setup codes
• Large arrows and OK, Back, Home, Mute, Volume and Play/Pause, all labelled
• Shows the TV's volume level
• Your phone's volume buttons change the TV volume
• Shortcuts to the apps installed on your TV (YouTube, Netflix, Prime Video and more)
• Search YouTube by typing or speaking
• More buttons when you need them: Source, Menu, Info, Channel, Rewind, Forward
• Remote buttons in the notification bar, a Quick Settings tile and a home-screen widget
• Turn the TV off, and on again with Wake-on-LAN (if your TV supports it)
• No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Open source.

Requirements
• A Samsung smart TV from 2016 or later (Tizen) on the same Wi-Fi as your phone
• The first time, choose "Allow" on the TV with your normal remote
