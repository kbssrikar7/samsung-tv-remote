# Privacy Policy - Remote for Samsung TV

_Last updated: 18 October 2026_

Remote for Samsung TV ("the app") is an independent, open-source remote control for Samsung
smart TVs. It is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Samsung.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The
developer receives no data from it.

## What stays on your phone

To work, the app keeps a few things in its private storage on your phone:

- the address, name and Wi-Fi MAC address of your TV (to reconnect and to turn it on);
- the pairing token your TV issues when you choose **Allow** on the TV;
- your settings (for example whether the notification buttons are shown).

This data never leaves your phone except to talk to your own TV, and it is removed when you
uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Network connections

- **Your TV, on your home network.** Remote buttons, volume, app shortcuts and discovery talk
  directly to the TV over your local Wi-Fi. Nothing goes to the internet.
- **YouTube searches.** When you search YouTube, your search text is handed to the YouTube app on
  your phone (or the YouTube website if the app isn't installed). From then on, YouTube's own
  privacy policy applies.

## Voice search

The microphone button uses your phone's built-in speech recognition (usually Google). The app
itself never records or stores audio; it only receives the recognised text.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Internet / network | To talk to your TV on your home network |
| Notifications | To show the remote buttons in the notification bar (optional) |
| Run at startup | To restore those notification buttons after the phone restarts |

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
