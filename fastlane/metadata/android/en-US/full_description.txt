A clear, easy phone remote for Samsung Tizen smart TVs, designed so anyone in the family can use it: big labelled buttons, a calm dark design, no ads, no account and no tracking.

<b>Remote</b>
<ul>
<li>Finds compatible TVs on your home Wi-Fi by itself; no IP address needed</li>
<li>Large D-pad with OK, Back, Home, Mute, Volume and Play/Pause, all labelled</li>
<li>More buttons when you need them: Source, Menu, Info, Exit, Channel, Rewind, Forward</li>
<li>Your phone's volume buttons change the TV volume, and the TV's volume level is shown</li>
<li>Shortcuts to apps installed on the TV</li>
<li>Remote buttons in the notification bar, a Quick Settings tile and a home-screen widget</li>
<li>Turn the TV off, and on again with Wake-on-LAN where the TV supports it</li>
<li>Reconnects to the same TV after the router gives it a new address</li>
</ul>

<b>YouTube</b>
<ul>
<li>Search YouTube on the phone by typing or speaking, and play the result straight on the TV: no on-screen letter grid</li>
<li>See what is playing, pause and seek from the phone</li>
<li>Favourites, recent searches, Play next and Add to queue</li>
<li>Works with TizenTube (via TizenBrew) as well as the regular YouTube TV app</li>
</ul>
YouTube features use YouTube's unofficial web and "Link with TV code" services. Linking is done once per phone with the 12-digit code from the TV's YouTube settings.

<b>Requirements</b>
<ul>
<li>A compatible Samsung Tizen TV (typically 2016 or later) with its local remote service enabled</li>
<li>Phone and TV on the same home network; guest Wi-Fi or device isolation can prevent a connection</li>
<li>The first time, choose "Allow" on the TV with your normal remote</li>
<li>Older TVs that need encrypted PIN pairing, including some 2016 models, are not supported yet</li>
</ul>
